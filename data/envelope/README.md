# data/envelope

The body envelope's inputs and results. Mixed: each entry says whether it is authored or generated and by what. Generated files are never edited by hand (`CONVENTIONS.md` sections 1 and 4). The library is `tools/envelope/`.

This list was one cell of the layout table in `CONVENTIONS.md` section 2 until 2026-11-11 (memo 61). The wording is that cell's.

## Authored

- `examples/`: the worked clearance example. Also the worked examples `pelvis_box_*`, `thorax_box_*` and `trunk_union_*` (poses in the model body frames since brief 7); brief 6: `thigh_components_*`, a candidate against the thigh's registry, with the committed `.out` of `fy envelope check`; `thigh_foreign/`, the same with a pelvis-fixed part swept over hip flexion; the arm examples `upper_arm_cylinder_*`, `forearm_box_*` and `arm_union_*`.
- `body_density.json` (authored by Joe): body fat fraction and lung volume with sources, the Blender volumes of v1, v2 and v3.
- `reference_densities.json`: Winter Table 4.1 length fractions and densities as Joe transcribed them, edition and page blank until he fills them.
- `dumas2015_table1_female.json`: the Dumas 2015 Table 1 abdomen and thorax landmarks, reference lengths and the uniform-density statement; the mass cuts read it.
- `components/<segment>.yaml`: the component registry, `components/1`; only `thigh_R.yaml`, a worked example of hand-typed primitives, exists.
- `reservations.yaml` (authored by Joe): the spine modules and the corridor, v2 reservations; schema `reservations/1`.

## Generated

- `trunk_registration.json`, by `fy envelope trunk`: brief 5 item 1.
- The trunk bodies' ledgers, silhouettes and result: `<pelvis|abdomen|thorax|head_neck>.yaml`, `_sections.csv`, `_sagittal.png`, `_coronal.png`, by `fy envelope build`; `trunk_bodies.json`, by `fy envelope gates`; `trunk_offsets.json`, by `fy envelope offsets`.
- `components/cad/<segment>.yaml`, by `fy cad pull` from the CAD sidecars, tracked; the registry loader reads it beside the authored file.
- `free/<segment>.yaml`, by `fy envelope free`: the free-space ledger at 10 mm stations.
- The arm bodies' ledgers, silhouettes and result: `<upper_arm_R|forearm_R|hand_R>.yaml`, `_sections.csv`, `_sagittal.png`, `_coronal.png`, by `fy envelope build`; `arm_bodies.json`, by `fy envelope gates`: registration, design numbers, closure and the share table.
- `reservations_result.json` and `reserved/<body>.yaml`, by `fy envelope reserve`: the placement search and the reserved registry per candidate body.
- `corridor_result.json` and `corridor/`, by `fy envelope corridor`: the sweep, the corridor registries and their free-space ledgers.
- `hip_registration.json`, by `fy envelope hip`.
- `v2_check.json`, by `analysis/makehuman_v2_check.py`: v2 against the rebuild, the volume attribution by region, the Ruler readouts.
- The thigh, shank and foot ledgers (`<seg>_R.yaml`, `<seg>_R_sections.csv`, silhouette PNGs), `leg_R_trial.json` and `sculpt_attribution.json`, by `fy envelope build` and `gates` and by `analysis/makehuman_attribution.py` (which needs MakeHuman's base.obj and targets, kept outside the repo).

## Not tracked

- `cache/` (gitignored): voxel fields and `inspect/`, the bundle `tools/landmark-picker` Inspect mode loads.

## Elsewhere

- `data/ansur2/ratios.json`, generated by `fy envelope ratios` from the raw ANSUR II female CSV, holds the landmark height ratios and girths.
