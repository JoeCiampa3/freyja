**Changes made.** Treadmill walking trials at 1.4 and 1.6 m/s only; steady-speed stride segmentation from the vertical force of
the instrumented treadmill; phase normalisation to 101 points; (Stage 2, later) zero-lag low-pass filtering, retargeting of
the OpenSim joint angles to Freyja's model, and load computation on Freyja's mass distribution. The dataset itself is not
redistributed; only derived summaries are.
