Read `CLAUDE.md`, `CONVENTIONS.md`, `docs/OVERVIEW.md`, `docs/memos/memo9-ankle-gain.md` (findings 1, 2, 6 and 7, and the T4 table), `docs/memos/memo5-stage2-t3c.md` (T3c.4 and T3c.5) and `docs/memos/memo6-stage2-close.md`, then the Decisions block below. Stage 2b is done (memos 7 to 9). This brief does not reopen the band, the model, the sheet, the template or the mapping. It authorizes one change to the retargeting (T2), and everything after that is regeneration and re-testing of what the old behaviour touched.

Why this brief exists: `memo9` found that `retarget.py` builds the root orientation as `R_stand @ (the dataset's pelvis rotation)`, so with `pitch_mean: measured` the model's pelvis pitch is the dataset's plus the standing pose's 2.608 degrees of forward lean, not the dataset's absolute pitch that the comment at `retarget.py:238` to `240` says it keeps. Freyja's legs sit rotated back by about 2.4 to 2.8 degrees from the dataset's through the whole stride, her ankle sits 42 to 44 mm further back relative to the hip midpoint at the ankle-torque peak (22 to 32 mm at every tenth of the stride), and A's ankle lever is 1.30 times the scaled dataset's. Cancelling the 2.608 degrees with the existing knob brought the ankle slope to 0.990 and 0.998. Every lever arm of every measured ground reaction in A and H carries the offset, and so does the CoP-to-ankle distance in B's polygon test.

## Decisions (Joe confirms by committing this brief; each line is a one-line revert if he disagrees)

- **The retargeting change is approved.** If T1 shows the composition was an oversight and not a recorded decision, `pitch_mean: measured` changes so that the model's pelvis pitch is the dataset's (anterior pitch is minus `pelvis_list`) plus `pelvis_pitch_offset_deg`, with no standing lean added. The numeric `pitch_mean` keeps its meaning (the cycle mean set relative to the standing pose's orientation). Roll and yaw are not touched.
- **Not through the spec.** `pelvis_pitch_offset_deg` stays 0 in `walk_treadmill.yaml`. The 2.608 degrees is the solved standing lean of the current model; it changes when CAD masses replace the anthropometry, so a constant in the spec would go stale without anyone noticing.
- **What the fix must satisfy.** (a) The model's world thigh and shank pitch equal the dataset's (hip angle plus the dataset's pelvis pitch, planar) to a tolerance you choose and justify; `memo9`'s counterfactual left -0.17 and +0.20 degrees on average. (b) The fix rotates the body about the hip midpoint, so the horizontal path of the hip midpoint is identical before and after to 1e-9 when `root_ref` is `hip_midpoint`. (c) The hip, knee and ankle joint angles of the retargeted cycle are unchanged to 1e-9. (d) The existing gait, retargeting, `mj_integratePos` and above-the-pelvis identity tests pass; any existing test whose expectation encoded the 2.608 degrees is listed in the memo, and its expectation is changed only for that reason.
- **Re-baselining is part of the fix.** `walk_reference.json` is rewritten, because the retargeting code changed with the snapshot unchanged and the baseline would otherwise fail its own check. The old figures stay in git history and appear in T4. If `A_filtered` does not meet the band after the fix, stop and report before writing any baseline.
- **B and H are not baselined, even if they now meet the band.** Report their numbers; Joe decides.
- **Sizing basis.** The dataset-direct row stays the sizing basis (ankle mean 102.5 to 108.9, 95th percentile 123 to 138, maximum 135 to 151 N m, supporting the frozen spec of about 103 N m at the mean) until Joe has read `memo10`. The regenerated `A_filtered` rows keep their provisional flag, and the envelope shows A and the dataset-direct row side by side with no new threshold or flag.
- **Spine plan (not final), carried forward.** v1 builds a multi-DOF spine and locks it during gait; the lumbosacral joint may instead stay unlocked and actuated for basic balance, with a fully rigid torso as the fallback. The three trunk modes: `rigid` is a locked spine; `counter_rotate` is an actuated lumbosacral roll and yaw cancelling the pelvis's roll and yaw by feed-forward; `first_harmonic` is a human-like reference point from the trunk IMU (a diagnostic, never a record mode). No mode is the v1 headline until Joe decides.
- **B's failure, carried forward and now provisional.** B failed the band, the polygon test and the dataset's moments, with a 66 mm backward CoP offset unattributed (segment masses about 18 mm, trunk-group placement none, trunk lean about 3 mm per degree). Every B number so far carries the 2.608 degrees, so T5 reruns them. Do not describe B's failure as a property of Freyja's mass distribution, her foot or the controller project.
- **Toe, carried forward.** The dataset's own foot is toeless (`mtp_angle` exactly 0). No proxy load is built from the force plate. `decisions/0001-toe-joint.md` stays `proposed`; cited literature values come from Joe and the planning session, not from Code. Its polygon-test evidence depends on T5(b).
- **Band, flags, schema, carried forward.** The band stays 0.9 and 15% of peak-to-peak. The pitch-flag rule (5 degrees, either speed) stays. `pose.com_x_rel_ankle_m` stays 0.0 as a design choice.
- **Hip extension requirement, carried forward.** The 22.0 and 24.1 degrees (95th percentile, from the vertical) come from the dataset's own IK (`hip_flexion + pelvis_list`), not from the retargeted model, so this fix does not move them. T4 confirms it. The 18.1 degree stop and the design pelvis pitch are Joe's decisions.
