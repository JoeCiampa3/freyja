## Context

This record closes rm.v1-upper-arm-char-contract. It decides what the upper-arm characterization file carries, how its interfaces are stated, and how a fall case reaches a segment whose skin it strikes but which does not house its zone, and it names the file's schema. The contract was settled in chat with Joe on 2026-11-13; this record writes it down. The file is produced under rm.v1-upper-arm-characterized by the schema, geometry, loads and file leaves, to a brief written after this record is accepted.

State of play:
- adr-0008 is the load basis: static gravity (D2), three provisional minimum-jerk swing rows (D3), the elbow's range (D4), the arm's fall energy of 430 J (D5), and the elbow stop and upper-arm column rated to 30 kN axial (D6f).
- adr-0042 sets the anchors: the elbow and wrist nodes on the section centre line (D1), their 7 mm and 5 mm bars (D2), the v3 sculpt (D3), the anterior-preserving roll (D4) and the forearm fixed at neutral (D5).
- Seven fall cases are recorded (memo 60). Each carries `zone_in`, the segment housing its zone (adr-0037 D3, adr-0044 D1). `seg.upper_arm`'s derived `fall_cases` holds lc.fall-hand-arm alone.
- Memo 59 finding 7: at the shoulder joint centre station, the thorax ledger's lateral ray is a cut plane, not skin. The shoulder's skin is in upper_arm_R, 34.3 mm beyond the shoulder joint centre at station 40. So lc.fall-shoulder-lateral, housed in the thorax, strikes the upper arm, and nothing in the thread says so. At that station `part.arm.upper_member` sits 19.3 mm inside the skin.
- The elbow actuator is not chosen (rm.v1-elbow-actuator-selected).
- The connector record is decision 0020 (rm.v1-upper-arm-connectors). It sits downstream of the requirements, the actuator and the shoulder concept.

Tags are as in 0008.

## Decision

**D1 Demand only.** The file carries what the world and the task impose on the segment:
- (a) static gravity under 0008 D2, swept over pose, at the design and nominal payloads;
- (b) the inertial rows S1 to S3 of 0008 D3, provisional as there;
- (c) every fall case that reaches the segment under D5, as energy, direction and contact station, with the 0008 D6 rating where one exists;
- (d) the ROM (D4), the available volume and the interface frames (D3).

It carries nothing from the actuator: no peak or stall torque, torque-speed curve, reflected inertia or anything read from `catalog/actuators/`. The actuator-peak structural case is the actuator's peak torque reacted through the upper-arm structure and its interfaces. It enters at rm.v1-upper-arm-requirements and at validation once the actuator is chosen. rm.v1-elbow-actuator-selected is not a blocker of rm.v1-upper-arm-characterized [Joe's ruling, 2026-11-13]. An actuator change then moves requirements, never the characterization.

**D2 Schema.** The schema id is `segment-characterization/1`, at `docs/schemas/segment-characterization-1.schema.json`. It is segment-generic, and the upper arm is its first instance.
- There is one file per segment, in the right-side CAD frame (CONVENTIONS section 3); the left side is its mirror.
- It is generated by `work.upper-arm-characterization` at `data/characterization/upper_arm.json` [design: path]. The schema leaf adds the CONVENTIONS section 2 row.
- It carries the banner keys and no timestamps.
- Every value is read from an address the work item consumes, or computed from those by a committed script. None is typed.

**D3 Interfaces are frames, not geometry.** Each interface is carried as three things:
- (a) *A frame.* The origin is a joint node, and the axes are the segment's frame under adr-0042 D4 (x anterior, y proximal, z lateral).
  - Elbow: the origin is the adr-0042 D1 elbow node.
  - Shoulder: the origin is the shoulder joint centre (the sheet's, through the trunk registration, 0042 D1), defined from the joint centre alone. No shoulder geometry is assumed, so rm.v1-shoulder-concept stays open.
- (b) *The envelope section at that station:* the skin section and the inner section at the 0007 D4 shell.
- (c) *The transmitted loads:* for every D1 case, force and moment at the origin, expressed in that frame.

Bolt circle, clocking, fastener count and size, dowels, compliance stroke and overload stop belong to decision 0020, which reads the requirement records. The characterization carries none of them.

**D4 Range of motion, elbow only.** Angles are elbow flexion, positive in flexion (`checks/joint_polarity.yaml`, the template's `range="-extension flexion"`).
- *Anatomical range:* −4.7° to 150° [source: snapshot elbow_extension and elbow_flexion, Soucie], read from the snapshot. The model's hinge limits are this range.
- *Mechanism travel:* −4.7° to 157°, as in 0008 D4. The flexion end is Magermans 2005 Table 4, the 95th percentile of maximum flexion in hair combing, 24 healthy women: a sample percentile, not a bound. The mechanism therefore reaches 7° past the model's flexion limit; ADR 0002 rule 6 allows a functional target above the anatomical row. This record restates 0008 D4 and does not amend it.
- *Loaded arc:* payload loads are claimed over Morrey's functional arc and self-weight alone outside it, as in 0008 D2 [source needed: the arc's bounds, as 0008 D2 notes].

Shoulder-side ROM waits on rm.v1-shoulder-concept, and the file carries none.

**D5 Fall contact.**

(a) *Field.* An energy-defined load case gains an optional `contact_in`: the segment whose skin is struck. When it is absent, the contact is `zone_in`. It is forbidden on the other source forms, as `zone_in` is. It means only that: housing, reserves (0037 D7), mass claims and budgets stay keyed on `zone_in`, so nothing is counted twice.

(b) *Derived relation.* Each segment gains a derived `fall_contacts`: every case whose contact is that segment. `fall_cases` is unchanged.

(c) *Reading rule.* A segment's characterization reads `fall_cases` together with `fall_contacts`. Contact alone would drop lc.fall-hand-arm from the upper arm as soon as that case's contact is set truthfully to `seg.hand`.

(d) *First use.* lc.fall-shoulder-lateral gains `contact_in: seg.upper_arm` [source: memo 59, finding 7].

(e) *Flag.* The flag belongs to a (case, segment) pair and lives in the file, never on the case, because one case can load one segment and only constrain another.
- A *structural load* passes through the segment's structure.
- A *clearance constraint* is a case where the shell grounds the impact into another segment's structure, and this segment's structure must stay out of the stroke.

Each flag cites its basis. For the upper arm:
- lc.fall-hand-arm is structural (0008 D6f).
- lc.fall-shoulder-lateral is a clearance constraint (0037 D5: the shell bottoms on torso structure). This is provisional, because the shoulder zone's stroke home is rm.v1-shoulder-concept's (0044 D3). If that record routes the impact through the humerus, the flag becomes structural and the file goes stale. Until then the file carries the case's station, normal and energy, and its clearance magnitude is open.

(f) *Schema change.* thread-record/2 is amended in place, because the field is optional and its absence reproduces the old meaning. A change that would invalidate any existing record still bumps the version. CONVENTIONS sections 3 and 8 change in the same commit. Joe approves the change through this record.

Gates for the schema leaf:
- G1. Every existing record validates unchanged under the amended schema, checked before lc.fall-shoulder-lateral is edited.
- G2. Every segment's derived `fall_cases` is byte-identical to before.
- G3. `seg.upper_arm`'s `fall_contacts` is [lc.fall-hand-arm, lc.fall-shoulder-lateral], and `seg.thorax`'s no longer holds lc.fall-shoulder-lateral.
- G4. A mutation test shows a `contact_in` that names a non-segment fails the build.

**D6 Error bars.** Every position and clearance in the file quotes its anchor's bar: 7 mm at the elbow, mostly anteroposterior, and 5 mm at the wrist (0042 D2). A value stated more finely is inside the anchor's uncertainty, and the file says so. Two consequences follow that 0042 does not state:
- (a) *Axes.* The upper-arm axis runs from the shoulder joint centre to the elbow node. So the elbow's bar tilts both interface frames by up to atan(7 / 255.2) = 1.6°, mostly in the sagittal plane [derived]. Loads resolved in those frames carry the tilt: at the 30 kN rating it is about 0.8 kN across the axis.
- (b) *Shoulder joint centre.* No record states its bar as an anchor; memo 54's 0.26 mm is raster repeatability, not anchor uncertainty. Shoulder positions are quoted with the bar "unstated", never zero, until a record states one.

## Consequences

- rm.v1-upper-arm-requirements must wait on rm.v1-elbow-actuator-selected, since the actuator-peak case enters there (D1). That is a roadmap batch for Joe. rm.v1-upper-arm-designed already waits on it.
- The loads leaf reads three cases: lc.fall-hand-arm, lc.fall-hand-arm-backward once it is recorded, and lc.fall-shoulder-lateral.
- The shoulder-lateral clearance stays open until rm.v1-shoulder-concept. The upper arm could not be finalized before then anyway (0044 D2).
- lc.fall-hand-arm keeps its default contact, the upper arm, which is not the skin it strikes. Under D5(c), setting it to `seg.hand` later moves nothing in the upper arm. The hand's characterization sets it.
- Load-path segments are seen by neither `zone_in` nor `contact_in`: the forearm carries the hand-arm load. The forearm's contract has to solve that, with a path relation or a wider reading rule. This record does not.
- The mechanism's 157° lies beyond the model's hinge limit, and the v3 sculpt has not been checked near full flexion. Soft-tissue clearance between the forearm and upper-arm envelopes there is a measurement for the geometry leaf, gated in the brief.
- No number in an accepted record moves, and no load-case value changes. lc.fall-shoulder-lateral gains one field.

## Rejected options, and why each one died

- *Actuator capability in the file.* It ties the file to an unchosen part, makes actuator selection a blocker, and goes stale on every actuator swap.
- *Bolt circle, clocking and fasteners in the file.* That gives decision 0020's values two homes.
- *A shoulder interface built from shoulder geometry.* It picks the shoulder concept by the back door.
- *Re-housing lc.fall-shoulder-lateral in the upper arm.* Housing is frozen (0044 D4). It would also move the thorax's reserve and claim, and double-count the claim if both segments held it.
- *Listing the case in `seg.upper_arm`'s `fall_cases`.* That corrupts the housing budget.
- *Stating the contact in the case's notes.* The thread cannot derive from prose.
- *A contact-only reading rule.* See D5(c).
- *The flag on the case record.* One case loads one segment and constrains another.
- *A bump to thread-record/3.* It rewrites every record for an optional field.
- *Shoulder ROM now.* It waits on the shoulder concept.
- *Positions stated finer than the anchor's bar.* They carry false precision.
