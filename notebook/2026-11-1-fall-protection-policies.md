# Context

This file serves as a living set of notes for how to implement fall protection within hardware design. Fall design is built in layers for redundancy. 

## 1. Shoulder Compliance and Arms as a Fall Break

One of the primary means of safely dissipating kinetic energy of the falling body is to use the arms to break the fall. 

## 2. Zones

The points that would be contacted first (bearing the brunt of the impact) during a fall are designated as zones. The zones at time of writing are the knee, hip, buttocks, back, and shoulder.
