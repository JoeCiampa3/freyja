In v1 although the acceptance test does not need most of these tasks: under the hardware rule, v2 tasks are control work, so the v1 hardware is sized for them now. Turning is the exception, in the acceptance course itself (the course turns at the far end of the rail). The hip yaw and trunk loads of a turn are not in the level-walking envelope.
