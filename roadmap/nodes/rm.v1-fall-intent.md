Joe's decision, at the level of protocol and principle. Whole-body trials run on the catch rig and nothing but the feet may touch the floor; the catch geometry (line length, slack and attachment) is a requirement on rm.v1-trial-rig-concept. The record also says what a catch failure means (v1 makes no survival claim for it), that the hardware is not credited with fall behaviour such as bracing or unlocking joints (v1 implements and tests none), and that v1 makes no tested fall-survival claim.

It does not fix magnitudes, cases or reservations. Those are rm.v1-fall-design-basis, which cannot be decided before the literature bracket exists.
