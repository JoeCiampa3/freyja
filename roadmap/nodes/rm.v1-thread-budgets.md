0036 requires every segment to carry its fall case or reserve the mass and space for it, and the hands and face are already reserved. Mass is a thread budget today (Stage 3a); volume is not, so a volume reservation would be prose. This puts it in each segment's budget as a number, on the same mechanism: claims against a provision, with reserved claims for hardware that does not exist yet.
