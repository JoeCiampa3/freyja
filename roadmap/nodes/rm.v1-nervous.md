Sector. Sensors, joint drivers and controllers (where they are not part of an actuator), the bus, the harness and the onboard compute hardware. Actuators and transmissions are Muscular; the power path and emergency stop are Metabolic; the software running on the compute is Mind. Actuators lead: the bus and controllers follow the lower-limb actuators under selection, which is why the architecture records wait on them.
