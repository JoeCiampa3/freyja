Sector. In-house processes and their qualification, the shop's metrology, and sourcing of bought parts. A process is qualified before any functional part is made with it: each fabrication node carries the qualification of the process it uses (rm.v1-dmc2-qualified for machined parts), wired when the node is created or its stub is expanded. Rigs and fixtures are not functional parts.

Approach so far, to be recorded in rm.v1-process-plan: parts are designed for the mill without a lathe, a soft rule revisited only if a part cannot be made otherwise; bearings, fasteners, shafts and anything else that costs about the same or less to buy are bought; machining is outsourced only if a bench test shows the shop cannot hold a requirement. Purchases are scoped to what a record requires, since the shop competes with actuators for budget.
