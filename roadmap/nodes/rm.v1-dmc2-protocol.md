Written before the first cut so the thresholds cannot be tuned to what the machine happens to do, the same rule as the acceptance protocol. Tests: positional accuracy and repeatability, flatness and surface finish, bore size, roundness and concentricity, and a cut test in the structural alloy. The thresholds are Joe's and live in the record.
